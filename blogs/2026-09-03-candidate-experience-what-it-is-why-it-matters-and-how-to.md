Candidate experience is one of the most direct levers an organization has over hiring outcomes and employer brand. A smooth, transparent, and respectful hiring process increases offer acceptance rates, reduces candidate drop-off, and builds a reputation that attracts stronger applicants over time. This guide explains what candidate experience means at each hiring stage, practical ways to improve it, how to measure it, and how platforms like Workable support a better process for both candidates and recruiting teams.
