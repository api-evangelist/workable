Workable has embedded AI across six core recruiting functions: job description writing, candidate sourcing from 400+ million profiles, resume screening and matching, a conversational recruiter assistant, interview question generation, and candidate rediscovery. Each feature is built into the core platform at no add-on cost. The platform is strongest for growing companies and recruiting teams managing high volume without dedicated sourcing tools or enterprise infrastructure.
