Hiring in technology is one of the most competitive recruiting environments. Senior engineers, product managers, and AI specialists are rare, actively pursued by hundreds of companies simultaneously, and rarely job searching. This guide explains how technology companies can compete for top talent using AI-powered candidate sourcing, structured hiring workflows that engineers actually use, integrations with Slack, Okta, Rippling, and dozens of other tools in their stack, and data-driven reporting for hiring decisions across teams and locations.
