Recruiting teams track time-to-hire, offer acceptance rates, source quality, and pipeline velocity. But most ATS platforms force a choice: reports that are easy to pull or reports that actually reflect how your company hires. This guide explains how Workable handles that problem with pre-built reports for everyday use, custom reports for specific needs, automated delivery to remove manual work, BI integrations for teams with existing analytics infrastructure, and AI-generated activity data that connects reporting to live sourcing and screening workflows.
