Artificial intelligence is now embedded in every stage of the hiring process, from automated job distribution and resume parsing to AI-powered candidate screening and agentic outreach. Organizations adopting the right AI recruiting tools are reducing time-to-hire, improving quality of hire, and eliminating repetitive manual work. This guide explains what AI recruiting tools do, what to look for when evaluating them, what compliance matters for hiring AI, and how platforms like Workable are shifting recruiting toward AI-native workflows with human control built in.
