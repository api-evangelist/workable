The expectation that HR and recruiting work happens only at a desktop is outdated. When hiring managers approve candidates from airport lounges and recruiters manage pipeline between meetings, the software has to deliver full functionality on mobile, not just technical accessibility. This guide explains what mobile accessibility means in practice, what to evaluate when comparing platforms, and how true mobile-first design affects time-to-hire and candidate experience.
